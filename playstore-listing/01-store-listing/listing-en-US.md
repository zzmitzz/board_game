## Full description (4000 max)

```
Turn any night in into a night to remember. ✨

DeepTalk is the conversation card game that gets friends, couples and new groups talking about the things that actually matter — and laughing a lot along the way. No awkward silences. No small talk. Just pass the phone, flip a card, and see where the conversation takes you.

🃏 PICK YOUR VIBE
Who are you with tonight? Choose a vibe and DeepTalk puts the right decks first:
• Friends & Close Friends — nostalgia, inside jokes and the stories you never told
• New Start — icebreakers for new groups, classmates, coworkers and first meetings
• Lover & Deep Romance — questions that bring couples closer on date night
• After Dark — bolder, spicier questions for grown-up nights (18+)

🔥 CHOOSE THE HEAT
Every deck has a Heat Level from Mild to Blazing, and every card is tagged Icebreaker, Deep or Intimate — so you always know how far the conversation will go. Start light, go deeper when the room is ready.

🎉 PARTY-READY GAMEPLAY
• Play with 2 to 20 players, each with their own avatar
• Swipe right when you answer, swipe left to forfeit
• Pass-the-phone mode keeps every card secret until the right player holds it
• Optional 30-second speed mode for fast, chaotic rounds
• Custom forfeit penalties — "take a sip", "do 10 push-ups", you decide
• One free skip per round for the question you really can't answer

🏆 DEEPTALK WRAPPED
When the game ends, see who won the night. Awards like Open Book, Mystery, Speedster and Brave Heart, a full leaderboard and the "card of the night" — ready to share with your group chat.

🌙 TONIGHT'S CARD
A fresh question every day. Reveal it, keep your streak alive, and get a gentle evening reminder (turn it off anytime).

✍️ MAKE YOUR OWN DECK
Write the questions only your crew would ask. Create custom packs with your own cards, cover images and hints — and play them anytime, even offline.

❤️ SAVE THE BEST QUESTIONS
Found a card you love? Save it to your Library or share it as a beautiful image.

🌍 CARDS IN YOUR LANGUAGE
Play in English or Tiếng Việt, and translate every card automatically into 13 languages — including Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Português, Русский, Türkçe, हिन्दी, Bahasa Indonesia, 日本語 and 한국어.

PERFECT FOR
✔ Game nights and house parties
✔ Date nights and couples' evenings
✔ Road trips, camping and dinner parties
✔ Team building and classroom icebreakers
✔ Getting to know someone new — or your oldest friends even better

No account. No sign-up. Open the app and start talking.

Some decks include mature themes and drinking-game penalties and are meant for adults 18+. Please play responsibly.

Download DeepTalk and start the conversation you've been waiting to have. 💬
```
